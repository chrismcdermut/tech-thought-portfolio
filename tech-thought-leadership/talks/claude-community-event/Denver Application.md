# Claude Community Event Application

## Program Overview

### About Claude Community Events
Thank you for your interest in hosting a Claude Community Event! This pilot program empowers passionate Claude developers to bring their local developer communities together to connect and learn from each other IRL.

**What you'll organize:** A 2-4 hour meetup for your local Claude developer community, featuring lightning talks, demos, and/or networking.

**What Anthropic will provide:**
- Sample event agendas
- Custom welcome video from Anthropic team members
- Anthropic team member Q&A over video call (pending team member availability)
- Claude stickers for attendees (shipped to you)
- Featured listing on Claude Community Events Luma calendar
- Financial support for venue and refreshments

**What you'll provide:**
- Secure venue and manage event logistics
- Lead facilitation and create an inclusive environment
- Managing event registration on Luma and check-in
- Sharing attendee list and event photos afterwards

**Note:** Claude Community Events are intended to stay focused on Claude, and are not for promoting other organizations or companies. Events should highlight that you are a Claude Community member and not an employee of Anthropic or formal representative of the company.

This pilot program is limited to selected developer community members as we refine the experience.
